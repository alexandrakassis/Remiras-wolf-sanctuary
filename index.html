About Our Sanctuary

Our Mission

Remira's Wolf Sanctuary is a non-profit organization dedicated to providing lifelong care for orphaned, injured, and displaced wolf pups. Founded by wildlife biologist Dr. Remira Thornwood, our sanctuary has become a beacon of hope for wolves in need.

We believe every wolf deserves a chance at life. Our team of dedicated caretakers, veterinarians, and volunteers work tirelessly to ensure each wolf receives the specialized care they need to thrive.

Baby Wolf Care

What We Do

🍼

Rescue & Rehabilitation

We rescue orphaned wolf pups and provide round-the-clock care including bottle feeding, medical treatment, and socialization.

🏥

Veterinary Care

Our on-site veterinary team provides comprehensive medical care, from routine check-ups to emergency surgery.

🌲

Natural Habitats

We maintain spacious, naturalistic enclosures that allow our wolves to exhibit natural behaviors and live fulfilling lives.

📚

Education & Outreach

We educate the public about wolf conservation and the important role wolves play in our ecosystem.
