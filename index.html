About Our Sanctuary

Meet Our Founder: Remi

Remira's Wolf Sanctuary was founded by 4-year-old Remi, a passionate young conservationist with a heart as big as a wolf pack's territory. After learning about the plight of wolves being targeted by Irish poachers, Remi knew she had to take action.

Despite her young age, Remi has an extraordinary gift for connecting with baby wolves. She believes every wolf pup deserves love, safety, and a chance to grow up wild and free. Her mission is simple: rescue wolves, build friendships with them, and give them the happy life they deserve.

"Wolves are my best friends," says Remi. "I want to save all of them!"

What We Do

🍼

Rescue & Rehabilitation

We rescue orphaned wolf pups and provide round-the-clock care including bottle feeding, medical treatment, and socialization.

🏥

Veterinary Care

Our on-site veterinary team provides comprehensive medical care, from routine check-ups to emergency surgery.

🌲

Natural Habitats

We maintain spacious, naturalistic enclosures that allow our wolves to exhibit natural behaviors and live fulfilling lives.

📚

Education & Outreach

We educate the public about wolf conservation and the important role wolves play in our ecosystem.
